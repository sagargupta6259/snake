HOW TO PLAY

  • Control the cyber snake using Arrow Keys or WASD (or touch swipe on phone).
  • Eat items quickly to build your Combo Multiplier before the combo bar drains!
  • Golden Apples grant 50 points and trigger a sparkle burst.
  • Frost Berries slow time down for tricky maneuvers.
  • Double Stars double all points earned for 10 seconds.
  • In Classic mode, avoid crashing into walls and yourself. In Portal mode, snake wraps through opposite walls!
